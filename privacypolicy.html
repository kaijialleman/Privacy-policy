4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Our current infrastructure uses Firebase services provided by Google LLC for authentication, database storage, and file storage. Google also provides the Google Cloud services described above. Google's data-protections are equivalent to the protections RunReport applies.

Information you mark as public may be visible to other Run Report users.

For safety and legal reasons

6. AI and Training Insights

Run Report uses BigQuery analyses to provide community-level fueling insights. Published patterns are shown only when at least 20 distinct opted-in participants contribute to a group and the data passes internal quality checks.

You may separately opt in to contribute eligible race summaries to Run Report's statistical analysis and custom fueling-model development. Participation is optional and does not affect your ability to use the app.

For this optional purpose, Run Report replaces your account identifier with a random pseudonymous participant key. This is pseudonymous data, not a promise of anonymity: notes are filtered and reviewed to reduce risk of identifying information, but some context may still be inferable.

You can withdraw at any time in Privacy Settings. Once withdrawal is recorded, Run Report stops exporting future eligible summaries and immediately begins deleting your rows from the research dataset.

After rows are deleted from BigQuery, Google Cloud's recovery systems may retain recoverable copies for a limited period. BigQuery's default time-travel period is up to seven days, and deleted data can remain in backups or logs for limited operational periods.

Withdrawal and deletion do not alter custom models that were already trained before withdrawal. We do not automatically retrain or modify those models. Data from a withdrawn participant may still influence a previously trained model in ways that cannot be fully reversed.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide the app and its features

When you delete your account, Run Report removes your Firebase account records and starts deletion of your active pseudonymous research rows. Deletion requests are processed promptly, but some data may remain in backups or logs for a limited time while systems complete removal.

While you remain opted in, pseudonymous fueling-research records have no fixed expiration date and are not automatically deleted on a schedule. They may be retained for ongoing statistical analysis and model quality monitoring.

Consent records

We retain a record of your consent status, policy version, and the dates you opt in or withdraw so we can enforce your choice and document how research records were authorized. This record is kept as a non-research administrative record.
