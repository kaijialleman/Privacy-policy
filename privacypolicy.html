4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or make information available in the following circumstances:

With service providers

We use third-party providers to operate Run Report, including services for authentication, database storage, file and photo storage, app infrastructure, diagnostics, and security.

Our current infrastructure uses Firebase services provided by Google LLC for authentication, database storage, and file storage. Google also provides the Google Cloud services described below. Google processes this information as a service provider on Run Report's behalf under the terms and data-protection commitments applicable to the Firebase and Google Cloud services we use.

  • When you upload a profile or activity photo, Run Report sends the selected image to Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch for an automated content check before storage. The in-app prompt identifies this image-processing transfer before it occurs. Images that fail the check are rejected.
  • If you opt in to fueling analysis and custom model development, Run Report replaces your account identifier with a random pseudonymous participant key and sends the listed eligible fields to Google LLC's Google Cloud services. BigQuery stores and supports analysis of the dataset, including statistical analysis such as clustering and dimensionality reduction. If you opt in, you also authorize the described future use of eligible data in Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform managed custom-training jobs to train and evaluate Run Report's custom transformer-based fueling-outcome classifier using fueling-history features and self-reported activity-rating labels. This trains Run Report's model; it does not send your data to the Gemini generative-model API. These training jobs are planned and are not currently active.

6. AI and Training Insights

Run Report uses BigQuery analyses to provide community-level fueling insights. Published patterns are shown only when at least 20 distinct opted-in participants contribute to a group, and each participant is weighted equally in the displayed average. The results describe associations and are not medical advice or a guarantee of an outcome.

You may separately opt in to contribute eligible race summaries to Run Report's statistical analysis and custom fueling-model development. Participation is optional and does not affect core app features. Only summaries created on or after your affirmative opt-in are eligible; summaries created before you opt in are not added retroactively. The research export uses the summary as it was first saved, and later edits do not update that research row. Eligible fields include race name, distance, date, start time, elapsed time when provided, visibility, self-reported activity rating, cheers count, fueling event types and timing, timeline slot offsets and count, event notes, and summary notes.

For this optional purpose, Run Report replaces your account identifier with a random pseudonymous participant key. This is pseudonymous data, not a promise of anonymity: notes are free text and could identify you if you include personal details. We do not include your username, account identifiers, account email address, profile photo, race photos, or other users' comments in the research export. We restrict access to the research dataset to authorized Run Report systems and personnel. A materially different service or purpose will require an updated disclosure and consent.

You can withdraw at any time in Privacy Settings. Once withdrawal is recorded, Run Report stops exporting future eligible summaries and immediately begins deleting your rows from the active BigQuery research dataset. If a service failure prevents deletion, the request is queued and retried until completed. To clean up delayed or retried export events, Run Report retains the random participant key in a server-only deletion queue for up to eight days after withdrawal, then deletes that key and queue record. Withdrawal does not remove your race summaries from Firebase because they continue to support your account's app features; delete those summaries separately in the app or request account deletion.

After rows are deleted from BigQuery, Google Cloud's recovery systems may retain recoverable copies for a limited period. BigQuery's default time-travel period is up to seven days, followed by a seven-day fail-safe period, so deleted data may remain in recovery storage for up to 14 days. Those copies are not available for ordinary analysis and are removed when the applicable recovery periods end. Run Report cannot promise immediate erasure from these provider-managed recovery copies.

Withdrawal and deletion do not alter custom models that were already trained before withdrawal. We do not automatically retrain or modify those models. Data from a withdrawn participant is not used for future exports or future model-training datasets unless that person opts in again.

Google Cloud recovery copies may remain for up to 14 days after deletion, information already viewed or shared by others may not be recoverable, and custom models already trained are not changed.

They may be retained for ongoing statistical analysis and model development until you withdraw consent or request account deletion. After withdrawal, deletion from the active dataset begins immediately; the server-only cleanup key may remain for up to eight days, and provider-managed recovery copies may remain for up to 14 days as described above.

Consent records

We retain a record of your consent status, policy version, and the dates you opt in or withdraw so we can enforce your choice and document how research records were authorized. A random participant key needed to complete withdrawal cleanup is kept server-side for up to eight days as described above.
