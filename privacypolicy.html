Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password information handled by our authentication provider
  • Profile photo
  • Authentication provider user ID
  • Account creation and update dates

You may also use certain features through anonymous authentication where supported.

Activity and training information

You may provide information such as:

  • Race and workout names
  • Distance, date, start time, and performance rating
  • Nutrition and fueling events
  • Food and drink entries
  • Timing relative to an activity
  • Training goals and personal notes
  • Favorite nutrition categories
  • Photos associated with activities

Social information

If you use our social features, we may collect and store:

  • Followers and following relationships
  • Friends and blocked users
  • Public activity summaries
  • Comments and replies
  • Cheers, likes, or other engagement actions
  • Reports submitted about users or content

Technical information

We may automatically receive limited technical information needed to operate the app, such as:

  • Authentication information
  • Device and app information
  • Error and diagnostic information
  • Basic usage information
  • Network and security information

We do not use this information to create advertising profiles.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Save and display your race, workout, and fueling data
  • Provide nutrition and activity pattern insights
  • Display your public profile and public activity summaries
  • Support followers, following, friends, comments, and cheers
  • Allow users to report or block accounts and content
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts
  • Improve app reliability, performance, and security
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Public Information

Some information is public when you choose to publish an activity or use social features.

Public information may include:

  • Username
  • Profile photo
  • Public activity summaries
  • Race or workout details
  • Nutrition timeline information included in a public summary
  • Public activity photos
  • Comments and engagement associated with public content
  • Follower and following counts
  • Public follower and following relationships

Do not include sensitive information in public activity summaries, comments, notes, or photos.

You can control whether new activity summaries are private or public using the app’s privacy settings. Information that has already been viewed, copied, or shared by others may not be fully removed or recoverable.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or make information available in the following circumstances:

With service providers

We use third-party providers to operate Run Report, including services for:

  • Authentication
  • Database storage
  • File and photo storage
  • App infrastructure
  • Diagnostics and security

Our current infrastructure uses Firebase services provided by Google. These providers process information on our behalf according to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

With other users

Information you mark as public may be visible to other Run Report users.

For safety and legal reasons

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or government request
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of Run Report, our users, or others
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, or violations of our terms
  • Respond to valid reports of harmful or unlawful activity

5. Nutrition and Health-Related Information

Run Report may allow you to record nutrition, fueling, performance, and activity information. This information may be sensitive.

Run Report is not a medical service and does not provide medical diagnoses or medical advice. Pattern insights are for informational purposes only and should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional when appropriate.

You should not use Run Report as a substitute for professional medical care.

6. AI and Training Insights

Run Report may provide automated activity or nutrition pattern insights based on information you enter.

Where the app provides an AI training or data-use consent setting, your choice controls whether eligible information may be used for that optional purpose. You can change your preference in the app’s privacy settings.

Automated insights may be incomplete or inaccurate. They should not be treated as medical advice or a guaranteed training recommendation.

7. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Access to private account data is restricted through authentication and database permissions. Public information is intentionally accessible to other users when you choose to publish it.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide the app and its features
  • Maintain account history

  • Resolve disputes

When you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify account information and associated activity data within a reasonable period, subject to technical limitations, legal obligations, backups, and information already shared publicly by other users.
